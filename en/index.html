NFC · RFID

Two frequencies, one antenna system.

We design antenna circuits that support both 125kHz RFID and 13.56MHz NFC.

03 / MIXED-SIGNAL & AFE

Sensors · Analog Circuits (Mixed-Signal · AFE)

Precision for the smallest signal.

We design circuits that read sensor signals reliably, even when they are buried in noise.

View design scope ＋
  • AFE (analog front end: the circuit that amplifies and filters a sensor's analog signal) · low-noise amplification · analog filter design
  • Data acquisition with a 24-bit ADC (a converter that turns an analog signal into digital values)
  • MCU-based ToF (time of flight: measuring distance or properties from a signal's round-trip time) and high-speed data conversion
  • Sensor interfaces for industrial, medical and semiconductor equipment

Related development: ultrasonic non-destructive concrete strength tester

03 / How We Work

From the first idea.
To the production line.

So design, validation and production never work in isolation,
we carry each stage's requirements into the next.

  1. 01

    Requirements Review

    Together we define product goals, operating environment and required features.

    Target specs · Project scope
  2. 02

    Circuit · PCB Design

    We design the circuit and board with noise and parts availability in mind.

    Schematic · PCB layout
  3. 03

    Prototyping

    We build validation boards and confirm they work in practice.

    Board build · Functional test
  4. 04

    Testing · Certification

    Compliance testing and debugging bring the product to completion.

    Reliability · Compliance tests
  5. 05

    Production Handoff

    We review manufacturability and components, then hand off to production.

    DFM · BOM · SMT Design for manufacturing · Bill of materials · Surface-mount technology

We agree on full development or support for specific stages during consultation.
