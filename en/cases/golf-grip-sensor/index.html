01 / Product

The club stays the same.
The data is new.

Ggrip is a smart golf club with sensors, wireless charging and AI built in. It tracks face angle, club path, speed and tempo in real time and connects to an app over Bluetooth for personalized coaching. It keeps the shape, weight and balance of a standard club, and won a CES 2025 Innovation Award in Fitness & Sports.

  • SWINGA swing trainer with swing path analysis and training modes
  • SWING PLUSA grip and swing trainer that analyzes grip pressure and swing path together
  • REGRIPA custom grip that brings Ggrip to your own club

02 / Design Scope

Inside the grip you hold.
Sensing, wireless and charging on one board.

The pressure measurement circuit, motion sensors, BLE radio and charging power fit on a single 4-layer build-up board.

  1. 01

    8-channel grip pressure

    Small signals from eight grip pressure channels are amplified, filtered and converted at 24-bit resolution.

  2. 02

    Motion and heading

    A 6-axis motion sensor and a 3-axis magnetometer measure the swing and the club orientation.

  3. 03

    BLE radio and antenna matching

    A BLE RF IC connects to the smartphone app, and the antenna matching was tuned on the prototype.

  4. 04

    USB-C charging and power

    One power management IC handles USB-C charging, battery management and the supply rails.

  5. 05

    Analog and digital separation

    The pressure section has its power and ground separated from the digital circuitry to keep measurement noise down.

  6. 06

    Haptics and a compact board

    A vibration motor gives haptic feedback, and the 4-layer 1.0mm build-up board fits inside the grip.
