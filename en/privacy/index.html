4. Deletion

Personal information past its retention period is deleted automatically every day. If you ask us to delete your information, we delete it without delay and irrecoverably.

5. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information at any time by emailing the officer below. We do not collect personal information from children under 14.

6. Security measures

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Stored information can be viewed only by the administrator with a password.
  • IP addresses are stored only as non-reversible values.
  • Download codes are not stored in plain text and work once, for 10 minutes.

7. Cookies

We use Google Analytics for visit statistics, which stores cookies in your browser. The cookies do not contain your name or email. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings without affecting your use of this website.
